## What is The Reformed Evangelical Movement?

A movement differs from an organization. A movement is a flaming fire and spiritual enthusiasm that kindles a group of people so that they become a powerful influence for other individuals to see, confess, continue and carry out the important task of changing the history. An excellent movement in the history always has a consistent theory, an agile strategy, a complete understanding, faithful followers, and an everlasting influence both in secular and spiritual fields. These elements can be seen clearly. Because of that, the Reformed Evangelical movement has to ask the Lord for help and blessing in important areas. We believed that the motivation for this movement is purely based on God’s calling and the needs of this era, as well as continuing living in faith as Christians in the history. So we see clearly that God blesses abundantly by leading and anointing, especially by reviving individuals who are kindled by the fire of the Holy Spirit. Soli Deo Gloria (All Glory be only to God).

## What is the content of the Reformed Evangelical Movement?

This movement covers two aspects. First, returning to the theological understanding based on the revelations from God in the Scriptures, which was pioneered by the Reformators, especially from the Calvinism school and its successors. With this strict theology, which came from the meanings stored in the Scriptures, the faith of Christians will be equipped and strengthened to face challenges of all eras. Second, kindling the spirit of evangelization and mobilizing Christians to directly proclaim the Gospel, which is the good news for all of humanity, introducing the power of salvation through the death and resurrection of Christ for the forgiveness of sin and peace between mankind and God so as to create a new life that glorifies God, witnessing for Christ, and proclaiming the Gospel. Accordingly, this movement pushes the church to root itself in the Word of God and to bear fruits in this world.
