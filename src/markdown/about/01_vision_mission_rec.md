title: Vision & Mission
language: EN
---

We call ourselves Reformed Evangelicals because we observe the Reformed theology and bear an Evangelical zeal. We believe the Reformed theology as a theology that is based on God’s sovereignty and exerts great effort to uphold the authority of the Scripture. The Evangelical zeal reminds us that as Christians, we have to be the salt and the light of the earth, love our neighbors who still live in sin, who need salvation through the blood of Christ. This we declare in our Confession of Faith, as well as Burdens and Prayers disclosed by Rev. Stephen Tong during the opening service of GRII in Jakarta.


The Reformed Evangelical Church of Indonesia (GRII) was established by Rev. Dr. Stephen Tong. GRII was founded to fight for a platform faithful to the Word of God. For that reason, GRII gives a very particular attention to the teaching of the Word of God from the pulpit. We commit ourselves to devote the pulpit as a platform for preaching the Word of God responsibly, that is based on a careful exposition of the Word of God and refraining from misusing the pulpit as means to further one’s own personal interests or to gain worldly riches and other secular matters.


The presence of GRII anywhere is expected to be a blessing for all Christians in the surroundings. Therefore, it is never on our minds that this church serves only one group of Christians, but we strive to make what we are doing to be a blessing for as many people as possible with the widest possible scope.
