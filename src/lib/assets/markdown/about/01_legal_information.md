In order to be legally recognized by the government of the Kingdom of The Netherlands
as a functioning church organization, we currently working to apply our church
as a foundation (_stichtingen_).

This step will allow us to serve our community with better responsibility under
the law. And more importantly, we are looking forward to build a trustable community
where the Gospel of our Lord might be flourished and glorified.
